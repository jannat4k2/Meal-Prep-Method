Air Fryer Garlic Knots

Keto-Friendly • Low Carb • Gluten Free

⏱️ Prep Time 15 min
🔥 Cook Time 10 min
🍽️ Servings 8 knots
🔥 Calories 250 kcal
★★★★★
4.8 (127 reviews)
Perfectly golden and ready in just 25 minutes!

These keto air fryer garlic knots are the perfect low-carb alternative to traditional breadsticks. Crispy on the outside, soft and chewy on the inside, and bursting with garlicky butter flavor. At only 3g net carbs per serving, you can enjoy these guilt-free!

Nutrition Facts (per serving)

250 Calories
20g Fat
12g Protein
3g Net Carbs

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • ¾ cup almond flour
  • 2 tbsp cream cheese
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • Fresh parsley for garnish
💡 Pro Tip: Use pre-shredded low-moisture mozzarella for best results. Fresh mozzarella contains too much moisture.

Instructions

1

In a microwave-safe bowl, combine mozzarella and cream cheese. Microwave for 1 minute, then stir until smooth and fully melted. If needed, microwave in 15-second intervals until completely melted.

2

Add almond flour, egg, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, and salt. Mix until a smooth dough forms. The dough should be slightly sticky but workable. If too sticky, add 1 tbsp more almond flour.

3

Divide dough into 8 equal portions. Roll each into a rope about 6 inches long, then tie into a knot. Tuck the ends underneath.

4

Preheat air fryer to 350°F (175°C). Place knots in basket without touching each other (work in batches if needed). Cook for 5-6 minutes until golden brown and crispy.

5

Melt butter in a small bowl, stir in minced garlic. Brush generously over hot knots immediately. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve warm!

Recipe Notes

Storage: Store in an airtight container for up to 3 days. Reheat in air fryer at 320°F for 2-3 minutes.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked knots on a baking sheet, then transfer to freezer bag. Cook from frozen, adding 2-3 minutes.

Dipping Sauces: Perfect with marinara, ranch, or garlic butter sauce!
