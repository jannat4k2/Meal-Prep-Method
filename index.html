Air Fryer Garlic Knots (Keto, Low‑Carb)

15 min prep · 10 min cook · 250 kcal

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups shredded mozzarella
  • ¾ cup almond flour
  • 2 tbsp cream cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning

Instructions

1. In a microwave‑safe bowl, melt mozzarella and cream cheese for 1 minute. Stir until smooth.

2. Add almond flour, egg, and seasonings. Mix until a dough forms.

3. Divide dough into 8 equal parts, roll into ropes, and tie into knots.

4. Preheat air fryer to 350°F. Place knots in basket, cook 5‑6 minutes until golden.

5. Melt butter, stir in minced garlic, brush over hot knots. Serve immediately.

Enjoy your guilt‑free, crispy garlic knots!
